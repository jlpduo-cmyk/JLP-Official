About JLP

Modern Rock from Vienna

JLP is a modern rock band from Vienna — born from a simple acoustic living room session and grown into a powerful full-band experience.

Founded by the siblings Jelena and Luka Petener, JLP have evolved into a powerful four-piece with a distinctive sound. Key releases include the 2025 single “Monster” and the 2026 track “Point Break”, both of which have received airplay on major Austrian stations FM4 and Radio 88.6, as well as UK-based Lyme Bay Radio. With a growing catalog and a reputation for high-energy shows, JLP are establishing themselves as a compelling new voice in the alternative rock scene.

"JLP isn’t trying to sound like anyone else. This track feels lived-in. It’s not aiming to be universal, it’s just honest. That’s what makes it land."

Music News Monthly

Their sound combines a tight, groove-driven rhythm section with gritty guitar tones and powerful vocals — creating a style that feels both raw and dynamic.

Lyrically, JLP captures the spirit of the moment, exploring social topics, personal experiences and stories drawn from real life.
