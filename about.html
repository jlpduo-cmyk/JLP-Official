About JLP

Modern Rock from Vienna

JLP is a modern rock band from Vienna — born from a simple acoustic living room session and grown into a powerful full-band experience.

Over the past years, the band has taken stages like Nova Rock Festival, Simm City, Szene Wien and other well-known venues across the city.

“Strong And Loud We Shout It Out”

Their sound combines a tight, groove-driven rhythm section with gritty guitar tones and powerful vocals — creating a style that feels both raw and dynamic.

Lyrically, JLP captures the spirit of the moment, exploring social topics, personal experiences and stories drawn from real life.
